## Clothing optimisation

- Optimise a selected High model by target polygon count or percentage.
- Choose conservative or aggressive High simplification per candidate.
- Compare the original High model with the candidate before applying any change.
- Preserve existing Medium and Low LODs during High optimisation.
- Back up the original YDD before applying reviewed changes.

## Safer generated LODs

- Medium generation now always uses border-preserving conservative simplification.
- Aggressive simplification is off by default and applies only to Low when explicitly enabled.
- Allow conservative Medium and Low to share a polygon count when protected topology prevents further safe reduction; a Low denser than Medium is still rejected.
- Add Close, Medium, and Far distance presets for realistic LOD review.

## Preview performance

- Render at reduced resolution while rotating or zooming, then automatically restore a full-quality frame when interaction stops.
- The representative 39,807-polygon hoodie interaction benchmark improved from 62 ms to 7 ms per frame.

The included Clothing Utility version is 1.7.0.
