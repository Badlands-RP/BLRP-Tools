# BLRP Tools v1.0.27

- Clothing Utility v1.8.4 expands YMT Settings to all custom clothing components
  and props, with per-slot audio choices and drawable/prop flags.
- Prop settings include alpha, decal, and cutout render modes.
- Head props include Cut/Scale Hair, with automatic updates to the prop
  expression and the creature-metadata YMT referenced by the addon. Hair must
  be rigged to MH_Hair_Scale for this setting to take effect.
- FEET retains shoe audio and adjustable heel height.
- Settings edits preserve prop IDs and unrelated clothing metadata, with
  backups before writing.
- Verified with a Release solution build, standard utility checks, and an
  isolated import/settings regression covering hat ID 065 and hair metadata.
