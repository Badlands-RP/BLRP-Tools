## Livery Studio Overhaul

### Added
- Real vehicle 3D previews, with or without a livery.
- Bulk livery creation and export queues.
- Dedicated modkit conversion with automatic master-list registration.
- Conversion-only exports for vehicles without artwork ready.
- Built-in Help tab.

### Improved
- Modern dark interface and simpler workflow.
- Searchable vehicle selection with expanded addon support.
- Automatic YFT building, export destinations, and metadata updates.
- Preview lighting, paint, body-part controls, backgrounds, and screenshots.
- Portable file locations with automatic discovery and manual overrides.
- Smoother preview camera controls.

### Fixed
- Incorrect modkit selection when switching between queued vehicles.
- Filename and label consistency with existing vehicle liveries.
- Lua entry placement, grouping, and formatting.
- Livery names, access groups, permissions, and public access handling.
- Custom backgrounds appearing in front of the preview vehicle.
- Export validation, cancellation, and rollback reliability.

### Preview limitations
- Offline previews may differ from FiveM rendering.
- Encrypted escrow models require a readable creator-provided asset or in-game viewing.
