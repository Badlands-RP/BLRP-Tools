## Livery Studio: Shared Modkits & Easier Exports

- Export liveries for vehicles sharing a modkit, with a manual override and a clear list of affected models.
- Keep existing livery rules for hiding vehicle extras when creating new liveries.
- Choose from all 19 business permissions, or enter a custom permission.
- See exactly which vehicle and artwork needs attention when a batch review fails.
- Keep carcols modkit IDs consistent during modkit conversion.
- Follow new step-by-step guides in Help and track preview loading with a progress bar.
