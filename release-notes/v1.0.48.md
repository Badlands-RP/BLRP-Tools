## Livery Studio: Browsing, Replacement & Paint

- Browse existing liveries in 3D with their in-game Lua names.
- Search livery selections by name, slot or filename, with clear availability labels.
- Replace existing livery artwork without changing its filename, slot, Lua name or access rules.
- Preview independent primary and secondary paint colours with Classic, Metallic, Matte, Satin and Chrome finishes.
- Redesigned Scene settings with separate Paint, Lighting and Scene pages.
- Fixed Pantor and Blimp3 previews, including livery support and camera framing when switching vehicles.

Paint colours and finishes are preview-only studio approximations; final rendering can differ in FiveM.
